Checking the book against the venue
Optional ITradingProvider facets for a venue that can be asked directly what it holds. A provider implements the ones its venue supports; the trading service probes each with AsCapability<T>() and calls it. A provider without a facet is not asked, and nothing is concluded for it.

Type	Members
IOpenOrdersSnapshot	Task<IReadOnlyList<VenueOpenOrder>> ReadOpenOrdersAsync(AccountId account, ct): every order resting at the venue on the account, read at the moment of the call; an empty list when the venue lists none. Throws when the venue could not be asked (a failed or cancelled request, an answer without the list): an unanswered read is never "no orders". TimeSpan ListLag (default 5 seconds): how long the venue's list can lag behind an order it just took or changed. For that long after an order was last stated, the list not showing it, or showing its old terms, says nothing. The trading service compares the answer with its own working orders.
VenueOpenOrder	readonly record struct (string VenueOrderId, OrderId? PlatformId, string? Instrument = null, OrderSide? Side = null, decimal? RemainingQuantity = null, decimal? LimitPrice = null, decimal? StopPrice = null): one order resting at a venue, as the venue itself lists it. PlatformId is the platform id the provider knows the order under, null for an order it cannot link to one (placed elsewhere, or before this session). The terms are stated only where the venue's list states them: null on a term means "the list did not say", never zero or none.
IOrderStatusRequest	Task<bool> AskOrderStatusAsync(OrderId orderId, ct): asks the venue what became of one order the provider holds. The trading service asks it for a working order the venue's open-orders list no longer carries, before it concludes anything from that. Three outcomes, never mixed. True: the venue knows the order, and when its answer differs from what the provider held (filled, cancelled, expired) the provider states it on EventEmitted as it states any other, fills included. False: the venue answered that it does not know the order, and nothing is stated. Throws: the venue could not be asked, or the provider holds no order under the id. An unanswered request is never "not known". An order the venue answers "not known" twice, a check apart, is ended by the service as Cancelled with OrderEndCause.VenueNoLongerKnowsIt.
IOpenPositionsSnapshot	Task<IReadOnlyList<Position>> ReadOpenPositionsAsync(AccountId account, ct): every position open at the venue on the account, read from the venue at the moment of the call and not from what the provider holds: one row per instrument, netted and signed as the provider states its positions, an empty list when the venue holds none. The read states and changes nothing. Throws when the venue could not be asked or did not answer the whole account: an unanswered read is never "no positions". The trading service compares the answer with the positions it holds. It is for a venue whose positions arrive on a stream; a venue that re-reads its positions itself every few seconds gains nothing from it.
IPositionResync	Task ResyncPositionsAsync(AccountId account, ct): reads the account's positions from the venue again and states every difference with what the provider holds on EventEmitted, the way it does after a reconnect: a position the venue no longer holds is stated flat, a changed one, or one the provider did not hold, with its size. Only a complete read states anything, and a position the venue's stream changed while the read was under way keeps the stream's word. Throws when the venue could not be asked or did not answer the whole account. The trading service asks for it when its own positions and the venue's read (IOpenPositionsSnapshot) keep differing.

Broker facts: SabrTrader.Pipeline.Venues.Trading.Facts
A venue-agnostic read model of what the brokers last said: every order and position each attached trading provider has stated, the most recent execution per position, and how far each provider's statements can be trusted. It holds statements only: positions come from what the broker stated, and a position the broker has not stated is unknown until it does. The trading service owns the one writer and feeds it every provider event on its emit pump, before any decorator or subscriber sees it, so a hidden account still has its facts. The host serves the read side as IBrokerFacts to its own order-management components (the ATM reads it). It is documented here because a trading provider's events are all it is built from: what your provider raises decides what it can prove (see Books-synced).

When a statement is proof
Arrival versions order one provider's statements. Each write records the provider's arrival version (AppliedVersion). It is the only ordering a consumer may rely on: a timestamp from one feed is never compared with a timestamp from another.
Confirmed. A position statement is confirmed when it was written at the provider's books-synced boundary (present in the book it published, or absent from it) or by an on-demand pull, and nothing has changed it since. A statement of another quantity, an execution (or a fill stated on its order), a connect, a disconnect or an account reset ends it; a re-mark of the same quantity keeps it. It is proof only while StatusOf the provider reads Current.
Flat by absence happens once per connect. At a books-synced boundary a pinned position absent from the published book reads flat and confirmed, and an unpinned slot that still states a position the book no longer holds goes back to unknown. Only a provider that is replaying reaches a boundary: one raised while it is disconnected, detaching or removed proves nothing and is ignored. There is no other flat by absence; otherwise a position is flat only when its provider says so.
The settle window. A provider's own position stream can trail a fill. Neither a boundary nor a pull confirms a position whose last execution arrived less than max(IPositionPropagationDelay, BrokerFactsTable.MinimumSettle) ago: the book's statement is written unconfirmed, a pinned position the book omits keeps its statement (unconfirmed) instead of reading flat, and RefreshPositionAsync returns false, so ask again later.
Pulls. RefreshPositionAsync asks the provider's IPositionRefresh facet and writes the answer confirmed. It returns false when the provider is not attached, has no such facet, is inside the settle window, or stated another quantity (or had an execution on the position) while the pull was in flight: that later statement stands. A failed pull throws what the provider threw. Never wait on it from the emit pump: the write it waits for runs there.
Providers and accounts that go away. When a provider is removed, its statements nothing pins are dropped and pinned ones stay, read against its status. On AccountStateResetEvent or AccountRemovedEvent, what nothing pins on that account goes back to unknown; a pinned slot keeps its statement (a pinned position unconfirmed) for the observer to settle.

CancelReplaceSequence holds the one decision tree for every such venue and CancelReplaceLedger<TReport> keeps every revision's fills counted once. The provider implements ICancelReplaceHost<TReport> over its own wire and book, keeps one ledger beside its book, and calls RunAsync on CancelReplaceSequence from its ModifyAsync. The host application implements none of these types. A provider whose venue modifies an order in place needs none of them.
